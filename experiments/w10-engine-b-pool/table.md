# w10-engine-b-pool: Engine B weight and pool window on public runs

Step 2 of the pool_s plan, public runs only, fixed before any QiSens result. Long phase clip: process_480p30 (default profile run, phases of about 1 min, like QiSens); short phase clip: shots_check_480p30 (phases of about 10 s); both GT files are team made. Rule: the pool window that leaves the long clip unchanged for the widest range of weights (no lower frame accuracy, no higher boundary MAE), with the weight at half of that range, provided it does not lower frame accuracy on the short clip. Chosen: engine_b.pool_s = 30 (unchanged up to weight 1.0; every other window breaks at 0.6 to 1.0), seg_a.engine_b_weight = 0.4 (short clip frame accuracy 30.0 to 46.1, MAE 1.65 to 2.50 s). Its pooled rate (0.56 peaks/min) is close to the long clip's 0.74 phase changes/min, against 7.8 raw. Engine B v0 gives no gain on the long clip at this setting; the single gain there (pool 60, weight 0.8) is isolated (45 and 90 at 0.8 are worse or unchanged) and not used.

Model engine_b/w10-engine-b-v0.pt; Engine A compare mode (CPU, in memory). B rate = Engine B peaks per minute, raw / pooled (as in Engine A's report).
